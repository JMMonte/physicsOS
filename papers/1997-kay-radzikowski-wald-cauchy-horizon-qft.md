# Quantum Field Theory on Spacetimes with a Compactly Generated Cauchy Horizon

## One-line summary

Kay, Radzikowski, and Wald prove rigorous QFT pathologies at base points of compactly generated Cauchy horizons, supporting chronology protection.

## What it actually shows

- The usual field algebra cannot be extended to certain Cauchy-horizon base points while satisfying local F-locality.
- Hadamard two-point functions become singular at those base points in the sense needed for semiclassical quantities to fail.
- Renormalized quantities such as field-squared or stress-energy expectation values become ill-defined or singular at those locations.
- The result supports Hawking-style chronology protection for compactly generated horizons.
- It does not prove that every constrained faster-than-c effective metric is impossible; it targets time-machine/Cauchy-horizon formation.

## Methods (briefly)

Rigorous algebraic QFT on curved spacetime, Hadamard-state analysis, and propagation-of-singularities theorems.

## Key equations / results

The load-bearing result is theorem-level: standard QFT structure breaks down at base points of compactly generated Cauchy horizons.

## Assumptions and regime of validity

- Linear scalar field on a spacetime with compactly generated Cauchy horizon.
- Initial globally hyperbolic region.
- Hadamard-state and F-locality conditions.

## Caveats / open issues

The result is a powerful warning against chronology violation, but it is not a constructive FTL mechanism and not a no-go theorem for every non-GR or quantum-gravity scenario.

## How it informs the mechanism-constrained FTL chronology claim

It strengthens the requirement that any viable mechanism-constrained FTL route must avoid Cauchy-horizon/time-machine construction, not merely hide the antitelephone in coordinates.
