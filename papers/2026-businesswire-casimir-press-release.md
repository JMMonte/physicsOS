# Casimir Inc. seed-round press release

## One-line summary

$12M seed-round announcement for a 5×5 mm semiconductor chip ("MicroSparc") claimed to deliver **1.5 V × 25 μA ≈ 37.5 μW continuously, with no degradation**, by harvesting energy from "quantum vacuum fields" via "engineered Casimir cavities."

## Claims, as stated

1. **Mechanism**: harvest energy from quantum vacuum fields using engineered Casimir cavities.
2. **Form factor**: MicroSparc chip, 5 mm × 5 mm = 0.25 cm² active footprint.
3. **Output**: 1.5 V at 25 μA continuous. Power = 37.5 μW.
4. **Areal power density**: 37.5 μW / 0.25 cm² = **150 μW/cm² = 1.5 W/m²**.
5. **Lifetime**: "no degradation and no replacement cycle" — explicitly continuous.
6. **Theoretical basis cited**: H. White et al., *Phys. Rev. Research* 8, 013264 (2026).
7. **Founder**: Dr. Harold "Sonny" White, ex-NASA Eagleworks (EmDrive / Q-thruster program).
8. **Funding**: Scout Ventures led; Lavrock, Cottonwood, Capital Factory, American Deep Tech, Tim Draper joined. Oversubscribed beyond original $8M target.
9. **Timeline**: commercial chip availability targeted for 2028.
