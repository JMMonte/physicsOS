# Physical Characterization of Metal-rich Near-Earth Asteroids 6178 (1986 DA) and 2016 ED85

## One-line summary

Near-IR spectra and prior radar evidence support 6178 (1986 DA) and 2016 ED85 as metal-rich NEAs, with 1986 DA the stronger case because it has radar confirmation.

## What it actually shows

- Metal-rich NEAs are a small subset of the NEA population.
- 6178 (1986 DA) and 2016 ED85 have spectra consistent with metal-rich bodies: red slopes, convex NIR shapes, and weak pyroxene absorption near \(0.93\,\mu\mathrm{m}\).
- The modeled surface composition is roughly \(85\%\) metal and \(15\%\) pyroxene.
- 1986 DA has prior radar evidence for high radar albedo, strengthening the metal-rich interpretation.
- 2016 ED85 lacks radar data; its metal-rich classification is therefore less secure.
- The authors estimate that the total Fe, Ni, Co, and PGM inventory in 1986 DA could exceed world reserves, but this is not a direct assay of PGM grade or recoverability.

## Methods (briefly)

Near-infrared spectroscopy compared against meteorite analogs, especially mesosiderites and bencubbinites, plus compositional modeling with metal and pyroxene mixtures.

## Key equations / results

- Best-fit mineral abundance: \(\sim15\%\) pyroxene and \(\sim85\%\) metal.
- Likely source region: outer asteroid belt via the 5:2 mean-motion resonance with Jupiter.
- Main mining-relevant inference: 1986 DA is a serious prospecting target for metal-rich material, but not a proven PGM ore body.

## Assumptions and regime of validity

Remote spectra and radar constrain surface/near-surface properties, not bulk PGM grade, regolith mechanics, recoverability, or industrial beneficiation. PGM abundance is inferred from meteorite analog assumptions, not measured in situ.

## Caveats / open issues

- No returned sample.
- No in-situ PGM assay.
- No demonstrated processing flow sheet for separating/refining PGMs on the asteroid.
- 2016 ED85 has no radar confirmation in the cited work.

## How it informs the PGM target-readiness claim

This is the strongest current primary-source basis for treating 1986 DA, and more cautiously 2016 ED85, as PGM prospecting candidates.
