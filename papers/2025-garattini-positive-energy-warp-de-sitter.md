# Positive-Energy Warp Drive in a De Sitter Universe

## One-line summary

Garattini and Zatrimaylov construct a special de Sitter-background warp bubble with non-negative Eulerian energy density and averaged energy-condition satisfaction, while still finding local NEC/WEC violations and severe practicality limits.

## What it actually shows

- A warp bubble embedded in de Sitter spacetime can have strictly non-negative Eulerian energy density under a highly specific condition: the bubble moves radially at the speed of cosmic expansion.
- The averaged weak and null energy conditions can be satisfied in the construction.
- Local NEC and WEC violations remain.
- The construction is not a practical interstellar drive: choosing direction requires starting at the center of the de Sitter universe, and long traversals occur on Hubble-time scales.
- The authors also prove a broader theorem: vacuum-energy perturbations that create underdense regions in every reference frame necessarily create local NEC/WEC violations.

## Methods (briefly)

The paper embeds a warp-bubble shift-vector construction in a de Sitter background and analyzes the stress-energy tensor and averaged energy conditions.

## Key equations / results

The load-bearing result for the claim is the conditional positive Eulerian density together with the explicit conclusion that local NEC/WEC violations remain and the solution is not practically directional or fast on human timescales.

## Assumptions and regime of validity

- Classical GR on a de Sitter background.
- Special radial motion tied to cosmic expansion.
- Vacuum-energy/dark-energy interpretation, not an ordinary positive-energy engineering medium.
- Local energy-condition violations are allowed in the construction.

## Caveats / open issues

This is a useful loophole paper but not a buildable FTL drive. It relies on cosmological background structure and does not provide a controllable reciprocal FTL channel.

## How it informs the FTL engineering claim

Mixed evidence. It shows the modern literature is exploring positive-energy-looking warp bubbles beyond flat asymptotics, but the construction itself does not support treating FTL as an ordinary engineering problem.
