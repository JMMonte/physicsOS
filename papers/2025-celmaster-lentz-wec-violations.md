# Violations of the Weak Energy Condition for Lentz Warp Drives

## One-line summary

Celmaster and Rubin directly challenge Lentz's positive-energy warp-drive claim, finding negative Eulerian energy density and derivation errors.

## What it actually shows

- The authors reconstruct Lentz-type rhomboidal-source warp-drive geometries and compute the stress-energy tensor from Einstein's equations.
- They report spacetime regions with negative Eulerian energy density, which is sufficient to violate WEC.
- They identify several errors in Lentz's derivation of WEC-compliance criteria.
- A modified geometry that better respects Lentz's intended constraints still violates WEC in the Eulerian frame.
- They emphasize that showing non-negative Eulerian energy would still be weaker than proving WEC, because WEC must hold for all timelike observers.

## Methods (briefly)

ADM/Natario-class geometry analysis, reconstruction of Lentz's source functions, and direct numerical/symbolic stress-energy computation.

## Key equations / results

The core result is a direct violation of the Eulerian energy-density condition for Lentz-type geometries. Since Eulerian observers are timelike, a negative Eulerian density is enough to refute WEC for that geometry.

## Assumptions and regime of validity

- Classical GR.
- Lentz/Natario-style warp-drive geometries.
- The paper is an arXiv preprint, not yet logged here as peer-reviewed.

## Caveats / open issues

The analysis is a direct rebuttal to Lentz, but should be weighted below peer-reviewed sources until journal review or independent replication.

## How it informs the FTL engineering claim

Negative evidence. It undercuts the strongest positive-energy superluminal soliton steelman logged in this claim.
