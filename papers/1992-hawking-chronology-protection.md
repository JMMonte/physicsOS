# Chronology protection conjecture

## One-line summary

Hawking argues that semiclassical backreaction and energy-condition constraints strongly support the conjecture that physical laws prevent closed timelike curves from forming.

## What it actually shows

- Finite-region chronology violation without curvature singularities implies a compactly generated Cauchy horizon.
- Under the analyzed assumptions, averaged weak energy condition violation is required on the Cauchy horizon.
- Even if quantum theory permits local energy-condition violation, stress-energy backreaction is expected to become very large near almost-closed timelike curves.
- The paper supports chronology protection, not practical FTL.
- It is not a theorem ruling out every quantum-gravity completion.

## Methods (briefly)

Semiclassical general-relativistic analysis of compactly generated Cauchy horizons, null geodesics, energy conditions, and backreaction.

## Key equations / results

The load-bearing result for this claim is categorical rather than a single number: closed-timelike-curve formation is expected to trigger strong semiclassical obstruction.

## Assumptions and regime of validity

- Classical/semiclassical general relativity.
- Causality violation forming in a finite spacetime region without curvature singularities.
- Compactly generated Cauchy horizon assumptions.

## Caveats / open issues

Chronology protection remains a conjecture. The paper supports treating time-machine production as a fundamental-physics obstruction, not as ordinary engineering.

## How it informs the mechanism-constrained FTL chronology claim

It is neutral-to-supporting background: chronology-safe FTL mechanisms should preserve a global time function or otherwise avoid Cauchy-horizon/time-machine formation.
